# 26: Covers open and are judged

**Spec:** `.scratch/nurse-rostering-v2/specs/cover.md`. Vocabulary: `GLOSSARY.md` (Cover). Reasons: `docs/adr/0011-a-cover-adds-one-nurse-and-the-first-acceptance-wins.md`. Where this ticket and those disagree, they win.

**What to build:** When leave takes a shift off the roster in force, a cover opens by itself, unoffered, and the leave form's answer points the manager to it. The manager can also open one by hand, from the new Covers page or from the roster grid's shift-day, for any shift-day of the roster in force today or later. The Covers page lists the month's covers, newest first, every state. A cover's page says what its shift-day still lacks (below its minimum headcount, no team leader, not both genders), from the solver's own check of the roster in force, and lists the staff nurses who could take the shift: off that day, within their dates, not on leave, allowed the shift, not held off by an accepted request, a manager with a nurse profile left out, and whose taking it newly breaks no hard rule, judged with the solver's own code in `web`'s process with her cell put in as a pin. The list is ranked by how far below her owed hours this month each stands, then least overtime this year, then name, with both figures beside each name, a mark on those whose taking it makes the shift-day whole, and a note where a manager's or a swap's pin to off would be replaced. A shift-day at its maximum headcount lists nobody and says so. The manager withdraws a cover, open or offered. A cover not taken expires when its date has passed on the hospital's clock: every page reads the date, and an hourly job stores it. A new version that puts one more nurse on a cover's shift-day, whatever made it, fills one waiting cover of that shift-day, oldest first, one per nurse added. Nobody is told anything in this ticket: no cover has been offered to anyone yet, and opening, filling and expiry stay silent for good. The audit log gets `cover.withdrawn` (the manager, target the unit) and nothing for opening, filling or expiry.

**What was found, and fixed.**

Leave now takes the unit's `FOR KEY SHARE` before any roster (`rosters.versions._key_share`), and opening by hand the unit's lock before the roster's: the documented order, unit then roster. `covers/tests/test_two_at_once.py` deadlocks without the second change.
- **A refused withdrawal lost the expiry it stored** (the refusal rolled it back); found by its test, fixed by a transaction of its own.
- **`e2e/ci.sh`'s first run failed one check,** `phone-375/manager.spec.ts`, "the manager's Swaps page … keeps within a phone's width", in `e2e/support.ts`'s `go`, not in this ticket's pages: `go` decides phone or desktop with one instant `isVisible()` on the Menu button, and on a page just signed into it answered false 800 ms after the heading showed (the trace), then waited on the wide header's hidden link. **Findings for you, not fixed:**
- **Swaps refuse a swap that brings a short shift-day nearer its minimum.** Their judging counts a hard violation as new when its text differs from the roster in force's, and a headcount's text changes with its count ("D = 0, want 2..3" to "D = 1, want 2..3"). So a swap moving a nurse onto a shift-day already short is refused as breaking its headcount, though it breaks it less. Covers compare a headcount by how far it is outside its range (`covers.judging.newly_broken`); swaps could use the same function. Not changed here: swaps' behaviour is ticket 20's, and no test of theirs covers it.
