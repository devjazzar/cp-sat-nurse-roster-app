# 27: Offer, accept, decline

**Spec:** `.scratch/nurse-rostering-v2/specs/cover.md`. Vocabulary: `GLOSSARY.md` (Cover). Reasons: `docs/adr/0011-a-cover-adds-one-nurse-and-the-first-acceptance-wins.md`. Where this ticket and those disagree, they win.

**What to build:** On a cover's page the manager ticks any of the nurses listed, all ticked by default, writes an optional note and offers it; on a cover already offered the manager adds more of the list, never takes one off. Each nurse offered is told and reads, on her own Covers page beside Swaps, the date, the shift, its hours and the note, and nothing of why the shift is short or who else was asked. She accepts or declines from her phone. The first acceptance takes the cover: judged again for her alone on the month as it stands, under the locks an approval takes, it makes a pinned cell of origin `cover` and a new version of origin `cover` whose reason names the taker, the shift and the date, in force at once; the unit's nurses are told the roster changed, the other nurses offered it that it is taken, and the manager that it was accepted. A later acceptance is refused as taken; a nurse who can no longer take it is refused in her own words and the cover stays offered to the rest. A decline is recorded and tells the manager, and an add does not ask her again; a cover everyone declined stays offered with nobody left. A withdrawal, a fill and an expiry now tell the nurses offered, never why. The manager's editor refuses a cell a cover's pin holds, as it refuses a swap's. An acceptance while a re-plan of the month runs makes the re-plan end outdated.

## Comments

**From ticket 26 (2026-11-07),** what was decided there that this ticket builds on. Ticket 26's comment has the rest.
- **Judging one nurse again.** `covers/judging.py` is the one place a cover is judged and worded. `CoverMonth(roster, day, shift)` reads the month once; `exclusion(hospital_id)` returns why she is out before her grid is checked, as a key (`not_on_grid`, `not_staff`, `works`, `not_in_month`, `outside_dates`, `leave`, `shift_not_allowed`, `request`), and `with_her(hospital_id)` returns her grid's check and the hard rules it newly breaks. The acceptance's refusals in her own words ("You are on leave on …", "This breaks a rule for you: …") belong in that module, worded from those. `judge()` is the manager's whole list; nothing of it is stored.
- **Newly broken.** A headcount is compared by how far outside its range it is (`newly_broken`), since the solver's text changes as one more nurse comes nearer the minimum; every other rule by the swap's key. Use the same function at acceptance, so the list and the acceptance cannot disagree.
- **Origins.** `VersionOrigin.COVER` and `PinOrigin.COVER` are not added yet. `covers/expiring.py` compares the version's origin with the literal `COVER_ORIGIN = "cover"`: point it at the new choice when you add it. Its rule is tested by calling `make_version(..., "cover", ...)` directly (`TestFilled.test_a_version_a_covers_acceptance_makes_fills_nothing`); replace that with the API's acceptance once it exists.
- **Locks and foreign keys.** A cover refers to its unit, and a foreign key is checked at commit with `FOR KEY SHARE`, which waits on anyone holding the unit `FOR UPDATE` (an edit, a rename holding every unit). So opening by hand takes the unit's lock before the roster's, and leave takes the unit's `FOR KEY SHARE` before the rosters (`rosters.versions._key_share`). Without it leave deadlocked with an edit and with a rename. An offer row refers to the cover and the nurse; the acceptance's pin to the unit, the nurse and the shift: hold those in the order the spec gives (catalogue, nurse `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, unit, roster, cover) and the checks at commit wait on nothing.
- **Where telling goes.** A withdrawal is `withdraw_cover` in `covers/api.py`; filled and expired both end in `covers.expiring._end`. Nothing is told there yet: add `COVER_STEP` to those three places.
- **The pages.** The manager's cover page says "It is not offered to anyone yet." where the ticks, the note and Offer go; `CouldTakeRow` carries `hospital_id`, which the offer can take. `COVER_STATE`, `coverName` and the hours words are in `frontend/src/lib/cover-words.ts` for the nurse's page. `ending` words in `unit-covers.tsx` (`endedWords`) already say "Taken by …, in version N".
- **The editor's refusal.** `rosters.editing.DraftEdits.refusal` already refuses any pin whose origin is not the manager's, with "This pin comes from an accepted {origin}, on {date}, so change it there.": with `PinOrigin.COVER` it reads "an accepted cover". Check that is the wording wanted.
