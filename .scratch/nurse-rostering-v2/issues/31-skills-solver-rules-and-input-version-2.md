# 31: Solver: skill rules and solve input version 2

**What to build:** The vendored solver understands skills. A version 2 solve input carries, per nurse, her skills with the expiry each counts to (or none); in the unit's block, its skill needs (shift, skill, weekday minimum, weekend minimum) and whether they are hard; in the hospital's block, which skills are required to work and the skill shortfall weight. The roster then meets each skill need or pays the weight per nurse short, keeps a nurse off every shift after a required skill of hers expired, and carries the hours she cannot work as debt. Precheck says beforehand what cannot be met; explain and validate name the two rules like every other. A version 1 input still loads as one with no skills, so stored inputs still explain and old months still close. Spec: `.scratch/nurse-rostering-v2/specs/skills-per-shift-day.md`; the reasons: `docs/adr/0010-skill-rules-in-the-solver.md`.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Two rules in the registry: **skill need** (hard or soft by the unit's flag, weight `skill_shortfall`, unit "nurse short of a skill need on a shift-day", active when the unit has any need) and **skill expired** (hard, unit "shift on a day a required skill has expired", active when any nurse in the month holds an expired required skill). Model, validator and explain follow the registry, as for every rule.
- [ ] A skill need counts nurses on exactly its shift on each free day against the weekday or weekend minimum; soft, the penalty is the weight per nurse short per shift-day; hard, the count must reach the minimum. Elapsed days are facts, reported apart.
- [ ] The bar: from the day after an expired required skill's last day the nurse works no shift. The most hours she can work excludes those days, so the hours her remaining days cannot hold are unreachable, exempt from the avoidable-shortfall floor and carried over as debt, by the existing mechanism. A nurse with no record of a required skill is not barred.
- [ ] Request version 2: nurse `skills` (skill name to expiry date or null), department `skill_needs` and `skill_needs_hard`, general `required_skills` and `weights.skill_shortfall`. The expiry is the one dated field accepted before the month; a date after the month is refused; a skill name must be in no list twice; a need must name a shift of the unit. Version 1 loads as version 2 with no skills, no needs, the flag at hard, no required skills and the weight at zero. The result's version is unchanged.
- [ ] Precheck lines: a need naming a skill no nurse holds on any free day; a need whose minimum exceeds the nurses who hold the skill and may work the shift, overall and per free day against those not on leave or barred; per nurse, each required skill expiring inside the month, with its last day. Barred days count like leave in the capacity sums.
- [ ] The validator's and explain's words give the rule, the shift, the day and the count short, in the solver's day numbering as today.
- [ ] Tests at the agreed seam: the rule suite in the constraint-verifier style (hard and soft; a need met, short by one, short by two; the bar with and without a record; an expiry inside the month counting before and not after; the hours carried as debt; elapsed days as facts), precheck lines, explain words, the loader on version 1 and version 2 and its refusals; one service contract test on a version 2 input. The solver's DESIGN notes gain the contract change.
