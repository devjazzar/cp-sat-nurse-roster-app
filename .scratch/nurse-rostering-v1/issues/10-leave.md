# 10: Leave

**What to build:** The unit manager records leave for a nurse: a leave type and one or more dates, visible on the nurse's month and in the unit's leave list; he can correct or remove a leave entry. Leave never appears to other nurses as anything but a day off.

**Blocked by:** 09 Unit rules and rostering attributes

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Record, edit and remove leave entries (nurse, leave type, dates), audited
- [ ] Unit leave list by month and per-nurse view
- [ ] A nurse sees her own leave with its type; colleagues see a day off
- [ ] API tests cover recording, scoping and visibility
